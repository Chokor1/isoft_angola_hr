# HR administrator guide

## Daily
**Angola HR → HR → Approval Inbox** — contracts, salary changes, advances, bank changes
and employee-submitted documents waiting for a decision.

## Hiring
Recruitment → accepted offer → **Create employee** (refuses a second employee for the same
applicant) → onboarding checklist → contract → salary profile.

**Ready for work** and **ready for payroll** are different questions and are answered
separately. Somebody can start on Monday without an IBAN; they cannot be paid.

## Contracts
Create individually, or **HR → Bulk Contracts** for many at once. The preview names every
employee that would be skipped (already has one) or blocked (inactive, no start date,
end before start), with the reason. Nothing is created until you have seen it.

Contract documents are generated from a template. Editing a template bumps its version and
never touches a document already issued.

Terms outside Lei n.º 12/23 produce a warning citing the article. They do not block —
the lawful ceiling depends on the legal ground for the fixed term, which the system does
not record. Confirm with your legal adviser.

## Probation
Reviews appear at 30 days. The manager gives a parecer; **you** confirm, extend or end it.
The decision is dated and attributed.

## Salary changes
Request → approve (somebody else) → apply. The effective date must be a payroll period
boundary; payroll cannot split a period between two salary profiles.

## Documents
Employee-submitted documents arrive **Pending Verification** and are not part of the
official record until verified. Reject with a reason — the employee sees it.
Mark a document type **Employee May Upload** to open it for submission.

## Performance
Create an **Isoft Performance Cycle** (period, template, scope, due date), preview, then
generate. Managers review, employees acknowledge, HR finalises. A finalised review can
support a salary-change request or a promotion recommendation — neither is automatic.

## Offboarding
**HR → Offboarding.** Produce the final settlement and close the contract **before**
setting the employee to Left — payroll cannot process an inactive employee.

## Monthly
Contract expiry, document expiry, master-data completeness, HR readiness. Alerts fire at
90/60/30/15/7 days and escalate to the line manager at 15.
